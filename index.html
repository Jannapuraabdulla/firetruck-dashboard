💡 How this works: Upload your QlikView material extract (Excel). The system auto-analyzes:
• Identifies pending materials (Open Qty > 0)
• Deduplicates by item code — clubs same item across multiple units into one row
• Multiplies Open Quantity × number of units (e.g., Open=4, Units=2 → shows 8 Nos)
• Combines PR + PO numbers into one column (format: "PO - 78906" or "PR - 45678")
• Major materials highlighted in red (chassis, pump, monitor, foam pump, generator, hose, etc.)
